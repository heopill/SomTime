Privacy Policy

SomTime (the "App") values your privacy and handles only the minimum information necessary. The App does not require sign-up or login, and because the developer does not operate any servers, the developer does not collect or store your personal information on any server. However, Google AdMob may process the information described in Section 5 to show ads.

1.Personal Information We Collect and Use

The developer does not collect any personal information that can identify you while you use the service (see Section 5 for information processed for ads). All of the information below that is created in the App is stored only on your device; it is never transmitted externally and cannot be viewed by the developer.

  • Your clock design, color, time format (12-hour/24-hour), seconds display, and PiP aspect ratio settings
  • The website address you entered on the Server Time screen
  • Your most recent server time measurement (the difference from your device's time and its margin of error)
  • Your server time display mode and milliseconds display settings

This information may be shared with the App's extension (widget) on the same device to show the clock in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and it is deleted from your device when you delete the App.

2.Network Requests for Server Time Measurement

When you use the Server Time feature, the App sends requests directly from your device to the website you entered and reads only the time information included in the response. These requests do not pass through any server operated by the developer, and no information is sent to the developer.

  • The App does not store or use cookies for these requests, and it does not store the response content.
  • As with any website visit, the website may receive connection information such as your IP address. That information is handled according to the website's own privacy policy.

3.Dynamic Island and PiP Clocks

The clocks shown in the Dynamic Island, on the Lock Screen, and in Picture in Picture (PiP) are all generated on your device, and the App does not use remote push servers. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, or other device permissions.

4.Information Processed When You Contact Us

If you send us an email through "Contact Us" in the App, the following information may be automatically included in the email body to help us respond to your inquiry.

  • Device model · iOS version · App version
  • Your device's language/region settings
  • Your email address, if you choose to send the email

This information is sent only if you send the email yourself and is used solely to review and respond to your inquiry. Whether to send it is entirely up to you.

5.Advertising and Third-Party Sharing

The App uses Google AdMob, Google's advertising service, to show banner ads at the bottom of the Home, Settings, Server Time, and Ticketing Practice screens. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may collect and process the following information.

  • Your IP address and the approximate location estimated from it
  • App- or developer-scoped device identifiers
  • Ad interactions, such as ad impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs and launch time
  • The App does not ask for permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your ad privacy consent, and you can change it at any time under "Ad Privacy Settings" in Settings.
  • For details on how Google processes this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Other than this, the App does not share your personal information with third parties and does not use any user behavior analytics tools.

6.Retention and Deletion

Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete the App. Emails received for inquiries are retained and deleted in accordance with applicable laws after the inquiry has been resolved. Information that Google processes for ads is retained according to the Google Privacy Policy.

7.Your Rights

You can delete the App's data stored on your device at any time by deleting the App. If you would like to access or delete information you sent us through an inquiry, please contact us at the address below.

8.Children's Privacy

The developer does not collect personal information from any users, including children. Information processed for ads is handled as described in Section 5.

9.Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated due to changes in laws or the service. We will notify you through the App when it is updated.
